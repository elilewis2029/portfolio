# UI/UX audit — 2026-10-08

Scope: every page of elilewisportfolio.info as a visitor and as the signed-in owner, on desktop (1280×900), iPhone-size (390×844, touch) and a 360-wide Android size, from a local production build of `main` (`54f76c7`) against the live Supabase project. Starting points were the two problems Eli named (no way back from a project page; sub-projects easy to miss). Everything destructive used a throwaway draft "AUDIT TEST nav throwaway", deleted at the end; no real project was edited, published, unpublished or deleted.

Guidelines applied, and how:

- **Nielsen's 10 heuristics** — mainly #1 visibility of system status, #3 user control and freedom, #4 consistency, #5 error prevention, #6 recognition over recall.
- **Apple HIG and Material** — 44 pt / 48 dp touch targets, no controls hidden under floating buttons, gestures that don't fight scrolling.
- **WCAG 2.1 AA** — 1.4.3 contrast (4.5:1 text, 3:1 large), 2.4.1 bypass blocks, 2.4.7 focus visible, 2.1.1 keyboard, 4.1.2 name/role, 2.3.3 reduced motion; plus 2.5.8 target size from WCAG 2.2 (24 px minimum) as the floor.
- **docs/RESEARCH.md** — the 30-second recruiter skim and the per-project template.

Severity: **blocks a task** · **confusing** · **cosmetic**. Status: **Fixed in PR 1** · **Prototyped** · **Open** (with a proposed fix).

Screenshots are in `docs/audit/ux/` (`before-*`, `after-*`, `proto-*`).

## What is already good (leave alone)

- **Structure.** Header → name, three tabs, résumé; footer with email, socials, Archive. A recruiter finds contact, role, process and result where they expect them (RESEARCH.md). Hero, outcome sentence, quick-facts strip, process photos with captions, result number highlighted, "What I'd change": the template is followed on every page.
- **Landmarks and skip link.** `header`, `nav[aria-label=Main]`, `main`, `footer`, one `h1` per page, a working "Skip to content" link, visible focus rings on every control (`:focus-visible` outline in the accent colour).
- **Images.** Every `img` has `alt` (caption or project title; decorative thumbnails use `alt=""`). Photos are 1600 px webp, lazy below the fold, with width/height so nothing jumps.
- **Motion.** Only a 0.6 s fade on page load and a slow hover zoom, both switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion` (verified: animation `none`, transition `none`).
- **No horizontal overflow** on any page at 360, 390 or 1280 px, visitor or owner.
- **Owner mode.** Draft cards carry a yellow "Draft" badge; owner-only controls are inside one dashed toolbar at the top of the page, not scattered; drafts are 404 for visitors with a helpful page ("It may have been unpublished or moved"); `/review` and `/add` redirect to sign-in with a `next` that brings you back. Delete asks first and names the photo count. Publish warns about `[TODO]` text.
- **Copyable email** in the header, "Copied ✓" feedback, falls back to `mailto:`.
- **Lightbox** opens from the keyboard (Enter on the photo), closes on Esc and returns focus to the photo that opened it (native `<dialog>`).
- **Empty states.** "Projects coming soon" / "Nothing here yet" / "No drafts" all exist and read naturally.

## Findings

### 1. No way back from a project page — blocks a task · Fixed
**Guideline.** Nielsen #3 (user control and freedom); #1 (where am I).
**Reproduce.** Open any project from /work. Look for a way back to the list.
**Seen.** Only the header tabs (`before-phone-project-no-back.jpg`). On a phone, after reading to the bottom of a 7,500 px page there is nothing but the footer.
**Change.** `app/work/[slug]/page.tsx`: a breadcrumb line "← Work" (or "← Archive" for archive projects) above the article and "← Back to work" after the last section, both 44 px tall, hidden in print (`after-phone-project-top.jpg`).

### 2. Current section not marked in the header — confusing · Fixed
**Guideline.** Nielsen #1; WCAG 2.4.8 (location).
**Seen.** Work / Experience / About look identical on every page.
**Change.** `components/NavTabs.tsx` (small client component, `usePathname`): the active tab gets `aria-current="page"`, a 2 px accent underline and darker text; /archive counts as Work. Links are 44 px tall.

### 3. Sub-projects are easy to miss — confusing · Prototyped
**Guideline.** Nielsen #6 (recognition rather than recall); RESEARCH.md ("decide in 30–60 s per page").
**Reproduce.** Open the Ely Tool page from /work (its card says "2 parts").
**Seen.** The only sign of part 2 is a 14 px grey "Part 1 of 2 · Ely Tool shop reorganization" line and two 22 px chips under the tagline, visually part of the header block and above the fold only on desktop (`before-desktop-series-strip.jpg`). The chip text is the full title, so on a phone the strip is two long pills and reads as tags.
**Change.** Three working prototypes on their own branches; see **Sub-project prototypes** below. PR 1 only makes the existing chips 30 px tall.

### 4. Sign in / Sign out lose your place — confusing · Fixed
**Guideline.** Nielsen #3; #4 (consistency: Save already returns to the editor after re-login).
**Reproduce.** On a project page tap the footer "Sign in", sign in. Or, signed in, tap "Sign out" on a project page.
**Seen.** Sign in → `/login?next=/` → home. Sign out → home. The owner has to find the project again.
**Change.** `components/SignInLink.tsx` carries `next=<current page>`; `signOut` (app/login/actions.ts) returns to the page the button was on unless it is owner-only (/review, /add, ?edit=1), then home. The login page gained "← Back to the site".

### 5. "+" button covers Sign out on phones — blocks a task · Fixed
**Guideline.** HIG (floating controls must not hide other controls); WCAG 2.5.8.
**Reproduce.** Signed in, on a phone, scroll any page to the footer and try to tap Sign out.
**Seen.** The 56 px "+" sits exactly over "Sign out" and the end of "Review" (`before-owner-phone-footer-fab.jpg`). `elementFromPoint` at the centre of Sign out returns the "+" button; a tap opens the sheet instead of signing out. The only working sign-out on a phone was from /review, where the footer wraps differently.
**Change.** `app/layout.tsx`: in owner mode the footer gets 96 px of bottom padding below `sm`, so its last row clears the button (`after-owner-phone-footer.jpg`).

### 6. "+" button over the editor and /review on phones — confusing · Fixed
**Reproduce.** Signed in on a phone: open `?edit=1`, scroll to the photo rows; or open /review and scroll.
**Seen.** The "+" sits on the right end of each photo's kind select (`before-owner-phone-editor-fab.jpg`) and over the Unpublish / Feature column of whichever /review row is at the bottom of the screen (`before-owner-phone-review-fab.jpg`).
**Change.** `components/owner/AddSheet.tsx` renders no button on /review (which has "+ Add" in its header) and while `?edit=1` is set (the editor has Add photos and Save). The sheet itself still opens from every other page.

### 7. Touch targets under 24–44 px — confusing on touch · Fixed
**Guideline.** HIG 44 pt, Material 48 dp, WCAG 2.5.8 (24 px minimum).
**Seen.** 25 filter chips at 22 px tall with 6 px gaps (`before-phone-work-filters.jpg`); header tabs 20 px; footer links 16–20 px; "Sign in" 38×16; series chips 22 px; /review "Edit" 21×16; buttons 34 px.
**Change.** Chips used as links are 30 px tall with 8 px gaps; header and footer links have a 44 px hit area with no visual change (`min-h-11` + flex); `.btn` is at least 40 px; the sheet's × is 44 px. For this to work `.chip`, `.btn` and `.input` moved into Tailwind's `components` layer so a utility on the element (`py-1.5`, `w-auto`, `border-accent`) can override them; before, the unlayered classes won and e.g. the editor's photo-kind `select` ignored its `w-auto`.

### 8. Filter state is invisible and hard to clear — confusing · Fixed
**Guideline.** Nielsen #1, #3.
**Reproduce.** Tap a skill chip, then "All".
**Seen.** The grid shrinks to two cards with no line saying why; "All" clears the category but keeps the skill; the only way to drop the skill is to tap the same chip again (a toggle nobody is told about). An empty result says "Nothing here yet", which sounds like the site has no projects.
**Change.** `components/ProjectGrid.tsx`: a status line "2 projects tagged prototyping · Clear filters ×" (`role="status"`), `aria-current` on the active chips, and "No projects match this filter." for an empty result (`after-desktop-work-filtered.jpg`).

### 9. Lightbox — confusing · Fixed
**Guideline.** WCAG 4.1.2 (name), 2.1.1 (keyboard), HIG (obvious dismissal); Nielsen #3.
**Reproduce.** Tap a process photo; try to scroll; look for a way to close it.
**Seen.** A nameless `<dialog>` with no control in it: Tab leaves the dialog, the only hints are a zoom-out cursor and Esc. A wheel or swipe scrolls the article underneath (scrollY 2731 → 3031 with the lightbox open) so after closing you are somewhere else. The caption is not shown (`before-phone-lightbox.jpg`).
**Change.** `components/Lightbox.tsx`: `aria-label="Full-size photo: <caption>"`, a 44 px × button top-right, the caption as a bar along the bottom, and `html:has(dialog.lightbox[open]) { overflow: hidden }` so the page stays put (`after-phone-lightbox.jpg`). Tap-on-photo and Esc still close it; focus still returns to the photo.

### 10. Dark-mode contrast — WCAG 1.4.3 fail · Fixed
**Seen (measured from computed colours).** Accent `#c2410c` on `neutral-950`: 3.82:1 (links, "All work →", category eyebrow, result number box 3.46:1). `neutral-500` on `neutral-950`: 4.18:1 (fact labels, captions, footer, "Part 1 of 2") (`before-desktop-dark-project.jpg`).
**Change.** `app/globals.css` under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`: the accent token becomes orange-500 (7.4:1 on the background), anything drawn on an accent background (`.btn-primary`, `.chip-on`, the "+" button, the skip link, the cover badge) uses dark text (7.4:1), and `.text-neutral-500` renders as neutral-400 (7.8:1) (`after-desktop-dark-project.jpg`). Light mode is unchanged.

### 11. Footer "Sign in" 2.6:1 — cosmetic · Fixed
`text-neutral-400` → `neutral-500` (4.7:1). It is meant to be quiet, not unreadable.

### 12. Owner actions give no feedback while they run — confusing · Fixed
**Guideline.** Nielsen #1.
**Seen.** Publish / Unpublish / Feature / Delete take about 0.9 s; the button does nothing visible until the page re-renders, so a second tap is tempting.
**Change.** `components/owner/ConfirmSubmit.tsx` now wraps every one of these (confirm message optional) and uses `useFormStatus`: the button disables and reads "Publishing…", "Unpublishing…", "Saving…" or "Deleting…" until the page refreshes.

### 13. Delete leaves no trace — cosmetic · Fixed
Delete redirects to `/review?deleted=<title>` and the page shows "Deleted “…”." (`after-owner-desktop-review-deleted.jpg`).

### 14. "+" sheet with an expired sign-in — blocks a save · Fixed (earlier audit #11)
**Reproduce.** Open the sheet, pick photos, write the note, let the session expire (cookies cleared in the test), Send.
**Seen before.** Redirect to `/login?next=/add`, photos and note gone.
**Change.** `app/add/actions.ts` returns `{ error: "signed-out" }` instead of redirecting (a thrown error would be masked in production); the sheet keeps everything and shows "Your sign-in has expired, so nothing was sent… sign in again in a new tab, then press Send" (`after-owner-phone-sheet-expired.jpg`). "Add photos" on a project page gets the same treatment with a one-line message.

### 15. "Done editing" with unsaved edits — confusing · Fixed (earlier audit #12)
`components/owner/DoneEditingLink.tsx` checks the browser-kept copy for this project and asks "You have unsaved changes. Leave anyway? They stay on this device and come back next time you edit." This works on iOS, where the browser's own leave-page prompt is ignored.

### 16. 404 page — cosmetic · Fixed
Title was "Eli Lewis — Engineering portfolio" (same as home); now "Page not found · Eli Lewis". The "About" button duplicated the header tab and became "Home".

### 17. Skip link — cosmetic · Fixed
`main` has `tabIndex={-1}` (and no outline), so activating the skip link puts focus in the content and the next Tab lands on the first photo, not back at the header.

### 18. "Currently —" — cosmetic · Fixed
The row is hidden for visitors while the field is a TODO; the owner still sees the highlighted placeholder.

### 19–20. "+" sheet name, close button; /review "Edit" — cosmetic · Fixed
The sheet is `aria-labelledby` its heading; × is 44 px. "Edit" on /review keeps its size but gets a 44 px hit area.

### 21. Printed part doesn't mention its series — cosmetic · Fixed
The print-only header line now reads "… · Part 1 of 2, “Ely Tool shop reorganization”: elilewisportfolio.info/work/detail-room-…".

### 22. Print length — cosmetic · Partly fixed
**Seen.** The three photo-heavy pages print at 4 letter pages each (Chromium PDF); the footer line said "one-to-two page PDF".
**Change.** Wording: "Print this page for a PDF copy (photos shrink to fit)."
**Proposal.** In `@media print`, cap the process grid at the first six photos (`.print-grid > div:nth-child(n+7) { display: none }`) and shrink photo height to 38 mm; that brings the Ely Tool page to two pages. Not done because it hides photos the owner chose to show; Eli's call.

### 23. Lightbox: tap closes, so no zoom on a phone — confusing · Open
A plan or drawing opened on a phone cannot be pinch-zoomed: the first touch on the photo closes the dialog, and the image is capped at 100 vw.
**Proposal.** Close only via ×, the backdrop or Esc (drop the `onClick` on the `<img>`), and let the image scroll (`overflow: auto` on the dialog, `max-width: none` on the image) so a 3000 px plan can be panned at 1:1. About ten lines in `components/Lightbox.tsx`; wants a real-phone check, which is why it is not in PR 1.

### 24. Phone keyboard and the Save bar — not testable here
Headless Chromium has no on-screen keyboard. Unchanged from the earlier audit: a 30-second check on Eli's phone (tap a caption, type, close the keyboard, Save).

### 25. Year / series order — Open
Unchanged from the earlier audit (#14): a non-numeric value saves as empty with no message. Proposal there still stands ("Year must be 4 digits" next to the field).

**Outcome (2026-10-08, after Eli reviewed the previews): C was chosen and merged**, with two more rounds on it: the overview became photo-first tiles with the first lines of each outcome, and the /work series tile became a static album-cover mosaic with a part-count badge (an auto-cycling photo was tried in between and dropped: WCAG 2.2.2, not skimmable). A and B are closed PRs kept as branches. DECISIONS.md item 33 records the result. The recommendation below is left as written.

**Recommendation: A.** It fixes the problem where it happens (a reader on part 1 never learns about part 2), keeps each outcome on its own page, which RESEARCH.md asks for and printing depends on, costs no extra click and the least code. B reads well on desktop but buries part 2 even deeper on a phone and doubles the print length. C is the cleanest information architecture and would be the right answer for a series of three or more parts with a story of its own; for a two-part summer job it adds a page that only repeats the parts. If Eli prefers C, keep A's card strip on the part pages as well; the two combine cleanly.

One small follow-up for A, whichever is chosen: the /work series card could list its parts as two text links under the cover so a reader can go straight to part 2 from the grid (about six lines in `components/ProjectCard.tsx`).

## How it was tested

- Playwright (chromium) against `next start` on port 3100; contexts for desktop 1280×900, iPhone 390×844 (touch, DPR 2) and Android 360×780.
- Owner session minted with the service key (`generateLink` → `verifyOtp`), cookies loaded into the context.
- Automated checks per page: landmarks, `h1` count, `img` without `alt`, horizontal overflow, every link/button under 44 px, tab order, skip-link focus, lightbox open/Esc/focus return, `prefers-reduced-motion`, contrast of every distinct text style in light and dark (canvas-normalised colours, backgrounds composited), print page counts via `page.pdf`.
- Flows walked by hand (scripted): breadcrumb, filter and clear, sign-in link → login → back link, sign-out landing from a project page and from the editor, Edit → Save → Done editing, Publish → Feature enabled → Unpublish, Delete → /review, "+" sheet open/Esc/expired send, Add photos, /review title links for a draft and a published project, draft slug as a visitor.
- PR 1 was re-run through the same scripts after the changes; `npm run lint` (tsc) and `npm run build` pass.
